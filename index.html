A2Appendix · Methodology & assumptions
① LTF cohort
A customer is in the long-term free-trial cohort if they had ≥3 months of $0 subscription. This is treated as the 12-month free-trial promotion population.
② Multi-orders
Accounts with multiple orders but no installed device are assumed to have installed on another account; their order history is preserved but not double-counted.
③ Active subscriber
An account is "active" in a month if it was either charged $23 (paid) or charged $0 with a device installed (free).
